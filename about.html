About OmniConvert

OmniConvert was founded on a simple principle: converting an image should not require sending private files across the internet to remote servers.

Why Client-Side Matters

Traditional image converters upload your files to remote cloud buckets. This creates three critical problems:

  • Privacy Vulnerabilities: Confidential screenshots, receipts, private photos, and business assets are exposed to server logs and third-party data breaches.
  • Slow Uploads: Large DSLR photos or multi-megabyte assets take valuable seconds or minutes just to upload before converting.
  • Server Outages & File Limits: Traditional websites impose restrictive file size limits and queue wait times.

How OmniConvert Solves This

Using modern HTML5 Canvas, WebAssembly, and native hardware acceleration built into modern web browsers, OmniConvert reads and re-encodes images entirely on your local machine. No data ever leaves your device, providing instant speed, zero upload delay, and ironclad privacy.
