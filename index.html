Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos or documents uploaded anywhere?

Never. Unlike other online converters that upload your photos to remote cloud servers, OmniConvert runs entirely on your local machine using client-side JavaScript. It is 100% GDPR and privacy compliant.

Why convert images to WebP?

WebP is a modern image format developed by Google that provides 30% to 80% smaller file sizes than JPEG and PNG without noticeable loss of image quality, boosting website loading speed and SEO.

Is there a file size or quantity limit?

There are zero server-imposed limits because the work is executed using your own computer's memory. You can convert high-resolution DSLR photos or large graphics seamlessly.
