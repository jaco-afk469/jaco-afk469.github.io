Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Core Philosophy: 100% Client-Side Processing

At OmniConvert (accessible from https://jaco-afk469.github.io/omniconvert/), your privacy is our top priority. Our service is engineered so that your photos, files, and graphic assets never leave your device. All conversions and compressions happen strictly in your browser using local client-side memory and HTML5 Canvas API. We do not operate storage servers, databases, or cloud processing pipelines for your images.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, passwords, or personal identity documents. When you drag and drop a file into OmniConvert, the file is read in memory by your browser and is destroyed when you close the tab or remove the image.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We use third-party advertising companies such as Google AdSense to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, or unique identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites across the Internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Local Storage

We use your browser's localStorage solely to remember your preferred language selection (e.g. English, German, Spanish). This information remains on your device and is never synchronized to any remote database.

5. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you are entitled to specific rights under the GDPR and CCPA. Because we do not store personal data or image files on servers, we do not retain or sell personal consumer records.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact Page.
